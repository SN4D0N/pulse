Privacy Policy — Pulse: Health & Recovery

Last updated: August 2026


Overview

Pulse is built on a simple principle: your health data belongs to you. This policy explains exactly what data Pulse collects, where it goes, and what it never does.


What Pulse Collects

Health data (Apple HealthKit)
Pulse reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep analysis, and respiratory rate from Apple Health. This data is used entirely on your device to calculate your daily Condition score and generate health insights. It is never transmitted to any server.

Nutrition data
Food and nutrition entries you log are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, this data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's end-to-end encryption. Pulse never has access to your iCloud data.

Workout data
Exercise sessions, sets, reps, and progression history are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account.

Medication data
Medication names, doses, and adherence records are stored only on your device. This data is never transmitted anywhere — not even when AI Coach is enabled.

Account information
Pulse does not require an account. Your name and preferences are stored locally on your device.


AI Features

Pulse uses a three-layer AI system. Here is exactly what each layer does with your data:

Layer 1 — On-Device AI (Apple Intelligence)
Daily insight cards and your morning briefing are generated by Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your data never leaves your iPhone for this feature.

Layer 2 — Siri Integration
Siri queries ("What's my Condition score?") are handled on-device via Apple's App Intents framework. No data is sent to Pulse's servers.

Layer 3 — Coach Chat (optional, opt-in only)
If you choose to enable the AI Coach, Pulse sends an aggregated summary of your last 7 days of health, training, and nutrition data to a secure server, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate a response. This includes:

  • Daily Condition scores and trends
  • HRV, sleep, and resting HR averages
  • Recent workout sessions (name, sets, duration, muscle groups)
  • Nutrition totals (calories, protein, carbs, fat — not individual food items)
  • Your goal and activity level

This feature is off by default. You must explicitly enable it in Settings. You can disable it at any time, and your conversation history is stored only on your device.

Raw HealthKit samples, individual food items, medication details, and your name are never included in any data sent to external services.


What Pulse Never Does

  • Sells your data to anyone, ever
  • Shows you advertisements
  • Uses third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase, Mixpanel, or equivalent)
  • Shares your data with third parties without your explicit consent
  • Collects data in the background beyond what HealthKit provides


Subscriptions

Pulse Pro subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Pulse never sees your payment information.


Data Deletion

You can delete all data Pulse holds about you at any time:

  • Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Pulse Data
  • This removes all locally stored data immediately
  • iCloud data can be removed from Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage


Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.


Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a material way, Pulse will notify you via an in-app notice on next launch.
